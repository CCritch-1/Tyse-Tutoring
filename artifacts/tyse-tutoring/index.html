Our Approach

What Is the Barton Reading & Spelling System?

The Barton Reading and Spelling System is one of the most widely respected structured literacy programs available for students with dyslexia and related language-based learning differences. Developed specifically for students who struggle with traditional reading instruction, Barton uses a carefully sequenced, multisensory approach — sight, sound, and movement together — to build lasting phonological awareness, decoding skills, and spelling accuracy from the ground up.

Unlike programs that expose students to material and hope repetition leads to retention, Barton is mastery-based: students do not advance until they have genuinely internalized each skill. This is a fundamental design choice. It means sessions move at the pace the student actually needs, not the pace a curriculum schedule demands. Progress is real, not performed.

Tyse is directly trained in the Barton methodology — not self-taught, not workshop-certified, but trained with hands-on practice in the actual system. That training shapes every session: the sequencing of instruction, the way errors are addressed, the way concepts are revisited and reinforced. Students who have tried other tutors and felt stuck often describe a noticeable difference in how the material finally starts to connect.
